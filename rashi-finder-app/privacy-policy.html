Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Rashi Finder app ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

The Rashi Finder app is designed to be lightweight and does not require account creation or login. The app may process the following information solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Name input: When you use the "Find by Name" feature, the name you type is sent to a third-party transliteration service (Google Input Tools) to convert it to Hindi script for zodiac sign calculation. This data is used only for that single request and is not stored by us.
  • Birth details: When you use the "Kundli / Birth Details" feature, the date, time, and location details you enter are used only to calculate your zodiac sign (Rashi) and are processed on our server for that single calculation. We do not permanently store this personal birth data tied to your identity.

2. How We Use Information

Any information entered into the app is used strictly to:

  • Calculate and display your zodiac sign (Rashi)
  • Generate a downloadable astrology card image, if you choose to download or share it

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app's core function.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Input Tools API - used for Hindi transliteration of names entered in the "Find by Name" feature.
  • WhatsApp - if you choose to use the "Share on WhatsApp" feature, the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; no data is sent to us in this process.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing the use of any information they process.

4. Data Storage and Security

We do not maintain a database of user names or birth details linked to individual identities. Basic anonymized rate-limiting data (such as IP-based request counts) may be temporarily stored for a short period to prevent abuse of the service, and is automatically deleted after a short time window.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Entertainment Purpose Disclaimer

This app is intended for entertainment and informational purposes only. Zodiac sign calculations provided by this app do not constitute professional astrological, medical, financial, or legal advice.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through RaipurTalks.com.
