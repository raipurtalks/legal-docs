Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Rashi Finder app ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

The Rashi Finder app is designed to be lightweight and does not require account creation or login. Depending on which feature you use, the app may process the following information solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Name input: If you use the "Find by Name" feature, the name you enter is used only to display your zodiac sign result for that session. We do not store this information.
  • Birth details: If you use the "Kundli / Birth Details" feature, the date, time, and location details you enter are used only to calculate and display your zodiac sign for that session. We do not permanently store this personal information tied to your identity.

2. How We Use Information

Any information entered into the app is used strictly to:

  • Calculate and display your zodiac sign (Rashi)
  • Generate a downloadable astrology card image, if you choose to download or share it

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app's core function.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on certain third-party services to provide its features, such as language processing and social sharing functionality. These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing any information they process.

4. Data Storage and Security

We do not maintain a database of user names or birth details linked to individual identities. Basic anonymized rate-limiting data (such as IP-based request counts) may be temporarily stored for a short period to prevent abuse of the service, and is automatically deleted after a short time window.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Entertainment Purpose Disclaimer

This app is intended for entertainment and informational purposes only. Zodiac sign calculations provided by this app do not constitute professional astrological, medical, financial, or legal advice.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
